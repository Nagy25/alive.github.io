Privacy Policy for The time clash

Last updated: September 22, 2026

smartech ("we," "us," or "our") created The time clash. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play the game.

Children's Privacy

The time clash is directed to children under 13. We do not ask players to provide a real name, email address, phone number, location, photos, camera access, microphone access, or payment details.

Parents and guardians may contact us to ask questions about their child's information or request deletion, as described below.

Information We Handle

To operate the game, we handle the following information:

  • An anonymous Firebase user identifier that lets the game save data without asking the player to create an account.
  • A player-selected gamer handle.
  • Game progress, completed and unlocked levels, virtual coin balance, and timestamps for saved data.
  • Advertising-related device and network information processed by Google Mobile Ads when a banner advertisement is displayed.

How We Use Information

We use the anonymous identifier, gamer handle, and saved game data to provide and maintain the game, preserve a player's progress, operate virtual coins, and prevent duplicate gamer handles. We do not sell personal information.

Service Providers and Advertising

We use Google Firebase for anonymous authentication and cloud storage. We use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner advertisements. These Google services may process information as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not independently share player information with other third parties except as needed to provide these services or where required by law.

Retention and Security

We keep game data while it is needed to provide saved gameplay, unless a parent or guardian requests deletion. Firebase access controls are used to limit player data to the associated anonymous user. No method of internet storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information we handle.

Deletion Requests

A parent, guardian, or player may request deletion by emailing ahmednagyhero25@gmail.com with the gamer handle and any available details that help us identify the saved game. We will delete the associated gamer handle, username reservation, game progress, and virtual-coin data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game or our data practices change. We will post the updated policy on this page and change the "Last updated" date.

Important Release Note

Before releasing The time clash as a child-directed game, smartech must configure AdMob requests for child-directed treatment and G-rated ad content. This policy does not itself change advertising settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact smartech at ahmednagyhero25@gmail.com.
