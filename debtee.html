Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 31, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. "Debtee" (the "App") is designed to respect your privacy and data security. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

1. Overview

Debtee is primarily an offline-first application. We do not store your personal financial records, debts, or loans on our servers. All such data is stored locally on your device.

2. Information Collection and Use

2.1 Personal Data

We do not knowingly collect or transmit your personal data (such as names, emails, or phone numbers) to external servers, except as described below for Advertising services.

2.2 Biometric Data

The App uses your device's biometric capabilities (Face ID, Touch ID, or Fingerprint) to provide a secure App Lock feature. This process happens entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your biometric data.

2.3 Photo Library Usage

The App requests access to your photo library to allow you to attach images to your records (e.g., photos of a person). These images are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your photos to any cloud server.

2.4 Advertising

This App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain data to provide personalized ads and analyze ad performance. This data may include:

  • Device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID or iOS IDFA).
  • Usage data and diagnostic information.
  • General location information (based on IP address).

For more information on how Google uses data, please visit Google's Privacy & Terms.

3. Log Data

In case of an error in the App, we may collect data and information (through third-party products like Google Play Services or Apple) on your phone called Log Data. This Log Data may include information such as your device Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing usage, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics.

4. Security

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

5. Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
